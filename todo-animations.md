# Animations

Ages: **K** kid, **T** teen, **A** adult. Kids goofy and clumsy, teens moody show-offs, adults dignified with rare goofy moments.

Already in: tail wag, head tilt, feste greeting, bored yawn, blink, gaze, expressions, tongue flick, slumber, hind-foot thump, leap, drill, wing fold, landing, ground breath, care reactions (feed, play, clean, exercise, groom).

## Can do today

Poses layered on the standing, sleeping or flying pose, played from a care reaction, a race moment or a random pick while the dragon is idle in the yard. The only new piece is that idle picker.

- Head-press / nuzzle into the keeper's hand, eyes closing (groom)
- Chin-up: lifts the chin to offer the throat (groom)
- Tongue-out blep, tip left out after eating or while dozing
- Slow blink back, gated on high bond
- Cat lick: licks a wing membrane, then the wing hand, then wipes the face with it
- Preening: nibbles along the wing fingers like a bird, then fluffs the mane
- Wet-dog shake after cleaning, rippling from the head to the tail tip
- Scratching behind the horn with a hind leg, wobbly in **K**
- Wing stretch: one wing out to full span, held trembling, refolded neatly
- Downward dragon: front low, rump up, long yawn
- Sniffing the ground, nostril flare and a small smoke puff
- Sneeze: soot puff, then looks startled at itself
- Hiccups with little smoke rings (**K**)
- Startle: jumps at a sound, then pretends it didn't
- Loaf: legs tucked, wings folded like a tent, eyes half-closed
- Stare off into the distance, mane moving in the wind (**A**)
- Paw at the screen: taps the glass (**K**)
- Toss and catch: flips food up and snaps it (feed)
- Messy eater: crumbs fly, licks its chops after (**K**, feed)
- Begging: sits up, wing hands together, big eyes (when hungry)
- Burp with a smoke puff after feeding
- Play bow: front down, tail high, chirp (play)
- Tug-of-war with the play item
- Bouncing on its tail like a spring (**K**)
- Flap-hop: tries to fly, hovers a few centimetres (**K**)
- Proud chest-puff after a win or a promotion
- Pout: lower lip out, puffy cheeks (**K**)
- Huff: two smoke jets from the nostrils
- Happy wiggle: the whole body shimmies nose to tail
- Purr / rumble: throat vibration, soft eyes (groom)
- Embarrassed: hides its face under a wing
- Frill / mane flatten when a minigame goes badly
- Tail as pillow: chin rests on its own tail while asleep
- Dreaming: twitching feet, little chuffs, a wing flutter
- Snoring with smoke rings
- Sleep-talking: mumbling chirps
- Waking stretch: one leg at a time, then a full-body shake
- Pre-race stretch: neck rolls, wing swings, a hop on the spot
- Psyching up: ground scratch like a bull, nostril steam
- Victory barrel roll in the air after the line
- Show-off loop at the ceremony
- Shake off after landing
- Panting after a hard race: tongue out, sides heaving
- Glance back at rivals mid-race
- Sore loser: kicks dirt, won't look at the winner (**T**, ceremony)
- Shell hat: a hatchling shakes a bit of eggshell off its head
- First steps: wobbly, plops onto its butt (hatch)
- First flame attempt: squeak, smoke puff, tiny cough (**K**)
- Voice crack: a teen roar breaks halfway
- Teen eye-roll and slouch

## Some supporting feature is missing

- Belly-up for scratches (**K T**): needs the groom reaction to target the belly; the roll itself is in "Too complex"
- Lick the keeper's hand: needs the hand reaching to the mouth, and a lick that smears the camera
- Cheek rub on a fence post or log: no rubbable props in the yard
- Claw sharpening on a post: no post in the yard
- Wing hug of a sibling, adult supervision, carrying a kid, play-fight: dragons don't share a shot or interact in the yard
- Good sport / snout bump with the winner: the ceremony has no dragon-to-dragon moments
- Rider and dragon fist bump: the rider isn't in the stable or on the podium dismounted
- Kneading, circling three times before lying down: dragons don't walk; they ease in place onto their bed
- Zoomies, pacing when neglected, tail chase spins: no yard locomotion or turning on the spot
- Fetch drop and nudge: fetch is a minigame with its own trip; needs a stick-in-mouth pose and a drop beat
- Pounce on a leaf or its own shadow, butterfly / firefly watch and snap: no ambient critters or leaves to target
- Stealing / hoarding shiny things: no loose items in the yard
- Lapping water: no drink action, no water bowl
- Food rejection: feeding always works, there are no preferences or a full refusal
- Sulk: needs a sulky state from neglect or a lost race to come back to
- Shivering when cold, fanning itself in the heat: no temperature
- Sunbathing: no time of day or sun in the yard
- Dust bath / rolling in dirt: dirt is a need, not something on the coat that rolling could add
- Polishing scales on a forearm: wants a visible shine or dirt state to change
- Mirror the keeper's head tilt: the camera doesn't tilt
- Rolling down a slope on purpose: the yard is flat
- Growth spurt trip over its own wings (**T**): no moment for the first days after aging up
- Catching snowflakes, shaking off rain, puddle stomping, leaf pile dive: no weather or seasons
- Cat vs dog variants of greetings: needs a personality gene

## Too complex

- Belly-up roll and lying on the back with legs paddling: the whole body turned over, wings splayed on the grass, ground contact for the back and wings
- Sleeping on its back, legs in the air (**K**): same flip, held while breathing
- Back roll in the dirt: same flip, plus coming back up
- Tail chase full spins (**K**): turning in place on planted feet with the tail in reach of the jaw
- Rolling down a slope: tumbling with ground contact all around the body
- Wing hug wrapping round another dragon or the keeper's hand: membrane collision with another body
- Carrying a kid by the scruff or on the back: one dragon attached to another's bones
- Play-fight between two dragons: two rigs reacting to each other's contacts
