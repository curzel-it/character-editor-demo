# Racetracks

Each track should favour a different kind of dragon, so upsets move around the season. Already in: **Valley** (open all-rounder) and **Canyon** (holding a line between walls).

| # | Track | Look | How it flies | Favours |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sea Stacks | Golden-hour coast, rock pillars in the surf | Low over the waves, slaloming the stacks | Handling |
| 2 | Glacier Fall | A blue ice tongue down from a peak | One long dive with gentle bends | Heavy, high top speed |
| 3 | Volcano Rim | A cone with a glowing crater | Climb the flank, circle the rim on strong thermals, dive down the lava side | Climbers |
| 4 | Waterfall Stairs | River terraces linked by falls | Drop after drop, each a dive, pull-out and slingshot | Acceleration |
| 5 | Storm Mesa | Flat tableland under thunderheads | Wide corridor, long straights, the pack stays together and bumps matter | Raw speed, weight |
| 6 | Elder Forest | Giant trees and clearings | Low gates in the clearings, skimming the canopy | Handling, small builds |
| 7 | Stone Forest | Dense karst pillars in mist | Many possible lines, lane choice over stats | Upsets |
| 8 | Sky Ruins | A ruined hill town: towers, bridges, arches | Tight turns, gates under arches | Handling |
| 9 | Fjord | A long sea inlet between steep cliffs | Big sweeping bends at full speed | Speed with handling |
| 10 | Dune Sea | Noon desert, heat haze | Thermals everywhere, the line you pick sets your speed | Light dragons, smart riders |
| 11 | High Pass | Climb to a snowy col, then down the other side | First half climbing, second half diving | Climbers, then divers |
| 12 | River Delta | Braided marsh channels | Flat, gates switch from channel to channel | Handling, lane choice |
| 13 | Aurora Lake | A frozen lake at night under the northern lights | Flat, ice arches; the first night palette | Even field |
| 14 | Cloud Sea | Summits poking out of a sea of clouds | High altitude, gates on the peaks, dives through the cloud deck | Divers |
| 15 | Terraced Hills | Cozy farmland terraces | Gentle switchbacks, easy to read; a Kids League showcase | Even field |
| 16 | The Crater Bowl | A stadium-like ring | The whole race in view of the crowd, finish on the crater floor | Season finale |

## Reversed layouts

Tracks with a one-way vertical profile change character when flown backwards: Glacier Fall becomes a long climb, Waterfall Stairs a climb up the stairs, High Pass swaps its halves. Flat tracks (Storm Mesa, Aurora Lake, River Delta) barely change reversed.
